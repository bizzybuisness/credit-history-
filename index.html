Privacy Policy

Last updated: 06 September 2026

CreditPulse ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of MSMEs and their owners ("you", "your"). This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

  • Business identity: Business name, type, industry, years in operation, GSTIN, Udyam registration, PAN, annual turnover.
  • Owner details: Name, email, phone, authorised signatory information.
  • Financial data: Bank transaction summaries (via Account Aggregator), supplier payment records, invoices, EMI and loan repayment data.
  • Documents: GST returns, ITR, bank statements, registration certificates — stored encrypted.
  • Usage data: Login events, feature usage, device information.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To build and maintain your business credit history and credit score.
  • To generate your Digital Credit Passport and share it with lenders only with your explicit consent.
  • To send alerts, recommendations and product updates.
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations (RBI, IT Act, DPDP Act 2023).

3. Data Sharing & Consent

We never share your data with any lender, supplier or third party without your explicit, granular, revocable consent. Every share is logged and time-bound. You can revoke access at any time from the Passport page.

4. Security

All data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). Bank data is fetched via RBI-regulated Account Aggregators — we never store your net-banking credentials.

5. Your Rights

  • Access, correct or delete your data.
  • Withdraw consent for any data share.
  • Request a full export of your data.
  • Lodge a grievance with our Data Protection Officer.

6. Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active, plus 7 years for regulatory compliance, unless you request earlier deletion.
